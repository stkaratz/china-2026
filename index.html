Beijing & Xi'an · −10 to +5°C

  • Proper winter coat (down or insulated), thermal base layers, hat, gloves and a scarf.
  • Warm, comfortable shoes for long walks on stone. Thick socks.
  • The Great Wall is windier and several degrees colder than the city.
  • Very dry air: lip balm and moisturiser.
  • Indoors is well heated, so wear layers you can take off.

Chengdu · 4 to 10°C, damp

  • The damp cold feels sharper than the number, and many restaurants, temples and shops have little heating.
  • Keep a warm mid-layer on indoors (fleece or light down) under your coat.
  • The Panda Base is outdoors in the morning: dress warmly for 2–3 hours.

Yangshuo · 6 to 14°C

  • Fleece plus a light rain jacket, and a small umbrella.
  • On the Li River boat or a bamboo raft it's windy and cooler than on land.
  • Comfortable shoes for cycling and walking between villages.

Shenzhen & Hong Kong · 13 to 22°C

  • T-shirt and light layers by day, a sweater or light jacket for evenings and air-conditioning.
  • New Year's Eve outdoors in Hong Kong is pleasant, around 15°C.
  1. Pack layers, not bulk. The trip runs from about −10°C to +22°C. You'll need the winter coat only for the first week, then carry it south.
  2. Air quality: still winter days in the north can be smoggy. Check the AQI (for example the IQAir app) and bring a few KN95/N95 masks.
  3. Rain gear: one small umbrella covers Chengdu and Yangshuo.

DiDi with two big suitcases

2 people · 2 × 20 kg

Choose

  • Premier 专车: large sedans such as Camry, Passat or Accord with a full-size boot. First choice. Costs about 1.5–2× Express.
  • 6-Seater 六座: van or MPV, always fits. Use it on train days when there is no time to lose.
  • Comfort 优享: a step above Express with bigger cars. Usually fits, not always, and not offered in every city.

Avoid

  • Discount Express 特惠快车: small electric cars with small boots. The most likely to leave you behind.
  • Carpool 拼车: shared with other riders, no room for luggage.
  • Taxi 出租车 in Chengdu and Xi'an: many run on gas with the tank taking half the boot.
  • Express 快车: a lottery between a big sedan and a small EV. Fine without luggage, risky with it.
师傅您好，我们两个人，有两个大行李箱（每个20公斤），后备箱放得下吗？谢谢！Send this in the DiDi chat as soon as a driver accepts: “Hello, we are two people with two large suitcases (20 kg each). Will they fit in the boot? Thanks!” If the reply is 放不下 (won't fit), cancel straight away while it is still free.
